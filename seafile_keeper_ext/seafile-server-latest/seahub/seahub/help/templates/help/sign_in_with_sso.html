{% trans "How to log in via MPG Single Sign-on on Keeper" %}

{% blocktrans %}MPG Single Sign-On (SSO) allows users to securely access Keeper using their institutional MPG credentials. With SSO, you can authenticate through your Max Planck Institute or affiliated MPG organization without using a separate Keeper password.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Below are detailed instructions on getting started with SSO.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Sign in to Keeper Using SSO (Web Interface)" %}

{% blocktrans %}For new users{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 1: Open Keeper.
Navigate to the Keeper login page in your web browser. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 2: Select Single Sign-On.
On the login screen, click Single Sign-On (SSO) located below the standard login fields.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 3: Select Your MPG Organization.
Choose your MPG organization from the list displayed on the login screen.
Click Next to continue.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Note: If your organization is not listed, please contact your local IT support team for assistance.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 4: Enter Your MPG Credentials.
Sign in using your official MPG institutional account.
Examples:

  • yourusername@maxit.mpg.de
  • Your institute/system password
{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Important: Use your official MPI or MPG credentials. Do not enter your Keeper password when using Single Sign-On.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 5: Complete Authentication.
Click Login to complete the sign-in process.
Once authentication is successful, you will be redirected to your Keeper account. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}For existing users{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}If you already have a Keeper account that uses email/password authentication, you can connect your account to MPG Single Sign-On (SSO) by following these steps:{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 1: Sign In to Keeper.
Log in to your Keeper account using your existing email address and password.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 2: Open Account Settings.
Click your profile icon in the top-right corner and select Settings. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 3: Connect SAML Authentication.
Scroll down to the Social Login section and click Connect next to SAML. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 4: Select Your MPG Organization.
Choose your MPG organization from the list displayed on the login screen.
Click Next to continue.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Note: If your organization is not listed, please contact your local IT support team for assistance.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 5: Enter Your MPG Credentials.
Sign in using your official MPG institutional account.
Examples:

  • yourusername@maxit.mpg.de
  • Your institute/system password
{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Important: Use your official MPI or MPG credentials. Do not enter your Keeper password when using Single Sign-On.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Step 6: Complete Authentication.
Click Login to complete the sign-in process. Once authentication is successful, you will be redirected to your Keeper account.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Important: After your account has been set up using SAML/SSO authentication, always use the same authentication method when signing in to Keeper. Switching between different authentication methods may result in account discrepancies, duplicate accounts, or access issues.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Troubleshooting" %}

{% blocktrans %}SSO Login Successful, but Your Libraries or Data Are Missing {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}If you successfully sign in using SSO but your Keeper libraries or data are missing, your account may need to be migrated to SSO.
Follow these steps.

  1. Log out of Keeper.
  2. Sign back in using your email address and password (not SSO).
  3. Navigate to Settings.
  4. Open Data Transfer.
  5. Click Data Transfer and complete the migration to your SSO account.
  6. Once the migration is complete, log out of Keeper.
  7. Sign back in using MPG Single Sign-On (SSO).
Your libraries and data should now be available.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Switch Back to Email/Password Authentication{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}If you would like to switch from Single Sign-On (SSO) to email/password authentication, follow these steps:

  1. Sign in to your Keeper account using MPG Single Sign-On (SSO).
  2. Click your profile icon in the top-right corner and select Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Social Login section.
  4. Disconnect SAML from Social Login and confirm the action when prompted.
Once completed, you will be able to sign in to Keeper using your email address and password. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Troubleshooting{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}If you receive the error message: "Failed to unbind SAML, please set a password first" you must first set a password for your account:

  1. Return to the Settings page.
  2. Scroll up to the Password section.
  3. Create and save a password for your account.
  4. Return to the Social Login section and disconnect SAML again.
After a password has been successfully set, you will be able to disconnect SAML and use email/password authentication for future logins. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Access Management{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}When your Keeper account is connected through MPG Single Sign-On, access is managed by your MPG organization.
Please note:

  • Access to Keeper is granted based on your active MPG affiliation.
  • If your MPG employment or institutional affiliation ends, access through SSO will be automatically revoked.
  • Your Keeper account itself is not automatically deactivated; however, you will no longer be able to authenticate through MPG Single Sign-On.
For questions regarding account access or organizational membership, please contact your local IT support team. {% endblocktrans %}
